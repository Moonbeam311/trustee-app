# ADR-10 — Executive Resume Engine

Defines the operational landing engine for IOS HOME.

Purpose:
Answer:
- Where did I leave off?
- What needs attention?
- What is blocked?
- What should I do next?
- Is the institution healthy?

Core panels:
- Continue Work
- Pending Reviews
- Execution Readiness
- Trust Health
- Matter Health
- Archive Integrity
- Compliance Alerts
- Recent Activity
- Recommended Next Action
- System Health

Rule: HOME should not duplicate every dashboard. HOME summarizes, resumes, and routes.

Accessibility

Hindsfoot's public presentation is designed for clear reading, keyboard operation, responsive use, and reduced motion.

Implemented practices

  • Semantic headings and landmarks
  • Skip links and keyboard-visible focus
  • Alternative text and decorative-image treatment
  • Responsive layouts and wrapping navigation
  • Reduced-motion support
  • High-contrast black, ivory, gold, blue, and white usage

Ongoing review

Accessibility requires continued testing with browsers, assistive technologies, content changes, and deployment configuration. A public feedback contact remains a required pre-deployment configuration item; no fake contact is shown.

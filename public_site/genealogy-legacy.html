A family tree shows connection. Hindsfoot helps preserve what those connections mean for records, responsibility, stewardship, and continuity.

Governed relationship evidence helps future stewards understand who was involved, what supported an assertion, which responsibilities existed, and what still requires review.

Governed progression

From a source to a continuity record—by deliberate review.

01

Source

A record, photograph, account, or other reference is connected and attributed.

02

Relationship assertion

A proposed connection is recorded without being treated as established fact.

03

Evidence review

Supporting and conflicting information remains visible for human evaluation.

04

Human confirmation

An authorized person explicitly records the reviewed status and remaining limits.

05

Continuity record

Confirmed context can inform future stewardship without transferring legal authority.

A relationship assertion remains an assertion until its supporting evidence is reviewed and its status is explicitly recorded.No stage advances automatically, and Hindsfoot does not independently determine genealogical truth.

Continuity with context

Help the next steward understand why connection matters.

A successor may need to know who was involved, how relationships were documented, which sources supported an assertion, what remained unresolved, who held a role, how responsibility moved, which property, trust, record, or institution was connected, and what future review remains necessary.

Relationship evidence can preserve context for responsible review. It does not automatically transfer authority, ownership, or entitlement.

Required limitations

Evidence must retain its limits.

  • Genealogical records may contain uncertainty or conflicting evidence.
  • Hindsfoot organizes records and source relationships; it does not independently authenticate DNA conclusions.
  • A genealogy record alone does not establish inheritance, ownership, citizenship, legal status, or entitlement.
  • Legal conclusions require appropriate professional and documentary review.

Legacy is connection made usable.

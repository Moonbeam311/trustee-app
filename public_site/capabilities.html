Principal capability

Work & Learning Hub

A guided workspace for asking questions, learning the system, developing a tailored program, and preparing work for deliberate review and confirmation.

Existing learning, discussion, workspace, note, and governed-review capabilities provide parts of this foundation. The unified Hub is the product architecture that brings those parts together while preserving the boundary between exploratory work and confirmed institutional records.

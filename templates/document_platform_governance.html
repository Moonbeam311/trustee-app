Document Governance Continuity

Document governance connects a document to directives, policies, relationships, evidence, execution sessions, document packets, and other governed institutional records.

A governance link preserves continuity between the document and the governing record. It does not by itself prove legal effect, authenticity, authority, execution, signature, delivery, filing, or completion. Verification, approval, lifecycle, execution, signature, delivery, filing, and evidence review remain separate governed actions.

Hindsfoot Guide

The Guide interprets the governed institutional record, explains context, identifies gaps, and recommends next actions while preserving operator authority over changes to the permanent record.

Governance boundary: The Guide may interpret and recommend but must not silently mutate permanent institutional records.

How Guide statements are classified

  • Recorded fact — information already present in the governed record.
  • System status — the current state reported by Hindsfoot OS.
  • Source-supported relationship — a relationship supported by identified evidence.
  • Inference — a reasoned interpretation that is not itself a recorded fact.
  • Conflict — evidence or records that do not presently reconcile.
  • Recommendation — a suggested next step for operator consideration.
  • Proposed action — a change or governed action awaiting operator review.
  • Operator-authorized institutional action — an action expressly approved by the operator for entry into the governed record.

Evidence discipline

Guide interpretations should identify their basis and distinguish what the record shows from what the Guide infers or recommends.

Genealogy evidence uses the governed progression: documented → corroborated → inferred → disputed → unresolved.

Conflict handling

Conflict detected → supporting evidence → proposed interpretation or correction → operator review → governed action → permanent audit record.

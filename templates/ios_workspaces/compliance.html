COMPLIANCE Workspace

Compliance governs review visibility, requirement provenance, lifecycle state, and institutional review history.

Compliance Review Records

Authorized operators may view currently available compliance information in read-only form. Compliance Review Registry creation, findings, disposition, approval, routing, closure, and remediation are not active.

Persistent registry workflows are not currently available. This workspace does not provide a control to create or activate Compliance Review records, and changing operator permissions does not make those workflows active.

Compliance Review Registry

A Compliance Review record does not itself prove compliance, legal sufficiency, approval, remediation, closure, or technical action. It is not legally conclusive.

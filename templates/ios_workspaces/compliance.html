COMPLIANCE Workspace

Compliance governs review visibility, requirement provenance, lifecycle state, and institutional review history.

Compliance Review Records

The Compliance Review Registry is read-only. Creation, findings, disposition, approval, routing, closure, and remediation are not activated in this milestone.

Compliance Review persistence is not currently available because the institutional foundation for this registry has not been activated. No review record was created, no migration occurred, and changing operator permissions will not activate the registry. Authorized institutional activation is required.

Compliance Review Registry

A Compliance Review record does not itself prove compliance, legal sufficiency, approval, remediation, closure, or technical action. It is not legally conclusive.

Reports Workspace Purpose

This workspace is a central read-only discovery and oversight surface. It helps operators locate authoritative institutional reports without moving governed actions or record-specific reporting out of their originating context.

The reporting chain is: Institutional Activity → Registry or Dashboard → Report → Evidence or Certification → Controlled Export or Archive.

Executive and Institutional Reports

Review institution-wide financial, portfolio, audit, and analytics information.

Financial Summary | Portfolio Dashboard | Portfolio Report PDF | Audit Dashboard | Audit Report PDF | Institutional Analytics

Export and Certificate Oversight

Review existing export records and institutional certificate records. Export creation, certificate generation, backfill, and mutation actions are not exposed from this workspace.

Export Center | Certificate Registry

Intake and Readiness Oversight

Review intake activity, export history, module completion, drafting readiness, review gates, and final-draft approval status.

Intake Dashboard | Intake Export History | Intake Module Ledger | Draft Readiness Ledger | Review Gate Ledger | Final Draft Gate | Final Draft Approvals

Governance and Evidence Oversight

Review governance activity, relationship audits, evidence registries, certification status, completion readiness, exceptions, archive intake, integrity, and manifest records.

Governance Dashboard | Relationship Audit Ledger | Evidence Export Registry | Evidence Certification | V2 Certification Record | Evidence Completion Gate | Evidence Exceptions | Archive-Intake Preview | Integrity Digest | Evidence Manifest

Contextual Report Boundary

Trust summaries, tax reports, K-1 reports, Form 1041 reports, trust ledgers, property timelines, execution manifests, execution ledgers, transfer audit trails, continuity certificates, instrument reports, evidence packets, and intake-detail reports remain attached to their originating institutional record.

Open the originating Matter, Trust, Property, Execution Session, Transfer, Intake, Certificate, Instrument, or Governance record to review those reports.

Controlled Export Boundary

Generated PDFs, CSV files, text exports, ZIP packages, print views, packets, manifests, certificates, and download artifacts may require record context, authorization, export policy, verification, approval, or prior workflow completion.

This workspace does not bypass those controls and does not create, approve, finalize, execute, or download record-specific artifacts without their required context.

Protected and Excluded Boundary

APIs, diagnostics, test routes, hosted repair tools, object-model status pages, builders, upload actions, backfill actions, recovery, permission reseeding, execution, approval, finalization, and destructive operations are intentionally not exposed here.

Their absence is an institutional safety boundary, not a missing feature.

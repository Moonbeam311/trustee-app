ARCHIVE Workspace

Review institutional archive readiness, evidence integrity, certification, continuity verification, and controlled backup access. Record-specific archive actions remain within the Matter, Trust, Execution, Transfer, Property, or Governance context that created them.

Archive Readiness Summary

The institutional archive chain is: Record → Evidence → Manifest → Integrity → Certification → Archive → Continuity → Recovery Readiness.

  • Evidence and certification surfaces are centrally reviewable.
  • Manifest and integrity records remain available for verification.
  • Contextual archive packets remain attached to their originating records.
  • Recovery execution remains protected and unavailable from this workspace.

Contextual Archive Records

These archive families remain within the institutional record or workflow that created them:

  • Execution Continuity: manifests, recovery documentation, replication status, and execution archive state.
  • Transfer Archive Handoff: handoff records, audit trails, corrections, and export packages.
  • Property Archive: archive packets, integrity review, manifests, continuity profiles, and finalization history.
  • Final Record Archive: final archive gates attached to the originating institutional event.

Open the originating Matter, Trust, Execution Session, Transfer, Property, or Governance record to perform contextual archive work.

Protected Recovery Boundary

Recovery execution, permission reseeding, freeze actions, finalization actions, restores, and destructive operations are intentionally not exposed in this workspace. Their absence is a safety control, not a missing feature.

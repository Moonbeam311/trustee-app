Relationship Guidance

A relationship connects one governed institutional record to another for operational review. It does not by itself prove legal effect, authenticity, authority, or completion.

Review relationship details before using lifecycle actions. Active, superseded, retired, and reinstated relationships remain preserved as institutional records.

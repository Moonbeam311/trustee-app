Evidence / Certification Export Continuity

Evidence exports preserve governance continuity records and export posture. Certification exports preserve institutional certification posture. Institutional Evidence remains part of Governance Continuity review.

CSV exports provide structured data for review, reconciliation, or external analysis. TXT exports provide human-readable certification or manifest records.

A download or export does not by itself prove legal effect, authenticity, authority, filing, delivery, or completion. Verification, approval, lifecycle, execution, signature, filing, delivery, archive, and evidence review remain separate governed actions.

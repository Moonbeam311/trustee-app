Relationship Guidance

A relationship connects one governed institutional record to another so an operator can see what a record authorizes, implements, supersedes, depends_on, governs, or references.

A relationship is a governed connection record. It does not by itself prove legal effect, authenticity, authority, or completion. Verification, approval, and lifecycle review remain separate governed actions.

  • authorizes: this record gives authority for another record or action.
  • implements: this record carries out another directive, policy, decision, or requirement.
  • supersedes: this record replaces or overrides a prior governed record.
  • depends_on: this record relies on another record before it can be understood or completed.
  • governs: this record controls or frames another institutional record or action.
  • references: this record cites or points to another record for context.

Outgoing relationships show what this record points to. Incoming relationships show what points back to this record.

Relationships may be active, superseded, retired, or reinstated through governed lifecycle actions where available.

# Test Artifacts

This directory is reserved for locally generated audit and verification reports.

Generated JSON reports are intentionally excluded from version control because
they are reproducible and may contain machine-specific paths, timestamps,
hashes, modification times, temporary locations, and environment metadata.

The durable audit controls are preserved in the committed audit scripts,
lineage registry, historical integrity manifest, control-coverage map, and
supporting documentation.

These local reports are evidence of a particular execution only. They do not
activate Compliance, modify institutional records, or replace the committed
successor audit suite.

Operators may retain, archive, or regenerate the reports locally as needed.
